ASSEMBLY AND MECHANICAL CHECKS STILL REQUIRED

- U601 (C128392) is confirmed NXP PCA9555PW,118 / TSSOP-24, and J601-J606
  (C131339) are JST B3B-PH-K-S(LF)(SN), 3 positions, 2 mm pitch. Live supplier
  CAD endpoints returned HTTP 403, so their CPL angle guesses (270 and 180
  degrees respectively) have not been independently pad-verified. Confirm
  U601 pin 1 at its PCB upper-left and J601-J606 pin 1 at the MIN/+5V end;
  check housing/key orientation as well as holes. AO3400A and B5819W cached
  supplier pad patterns support the new MOSFET/diode orientations.
- The raw CPL audit deliberately reports pad-center differences; these do
  not by themselves prove a footprint error. Large drain/exposed pads can
  contain several supplier lead positions. For example, the relay supplier
  CAD differs from the official hole drawing, while the PCB's 2 mm common-
  to-coil-row offset agrees with Omron's drawing. Do not blindly fit every
  supplier pad center or change copper from the raw residual list.
- Verify polarity for D2, electrolytics, Q1 and the actual XT60 part. Catalogue
  C98733 is the XT60-M connector; have the assembler confirm the physical
  termination fits these holes and their assembly process, or hand-fit J1.
- Actual OLED holes H5/H6: (67.85,105.30) and (91.15,105.30) mm. Horizontal
  spacing is 23.30 mm; hole row is 23.80 mm below the header row at Y=81.50.
  The user's earlier module description specified 23.0 mm spacing and Y=104.0
  holes. Those are different mechanical assumptions; a physical module or
  authoritative drawing is needed to approve fit. Holes were left untouched
  because MC096GW/JMD/EastRising names do not establish interchangeable PCBs.

ELECTRICAL LIMITS AND TEST PLAN

The compact limit inputs support dry contacts/3.3 V logic ONLY. There is no
industrial 12/24 V front end. The eight channels have filtering but no universal
external-input surge immunity. Use firmware debounce and direction-aware
limits; permit a controlled move away from an active limit. A broken NC wire
can be treated as a limit fault. I2C sensing is not hardware overtravel shutdown.

U601 cold-start outputs are inputs. External 1k command pulldowns keep AUX gates
off. Firmware must first configure ports as inputs, load Port 1 latch 0x0C,
then set Port 1 configuration 0xF0. Port 0 stays 0xFF for sensing. Grounded
Port 1 bits 5..7 must remain inputs. ESP32-only resets do not reset the
expander; test warm resets and I2C failures, not just cold boot.

Opening J102 drives the hardware stop net low, sleeps DC drivers and gates
onboard/external stepper enables. The output pullups select disabled active-
low enable levels. External driver voltage/current/polarity must match this
3.3 V interface. Relays and AUX outputs are not cut by this loop. Confirm the
stop behavior with the actual drivers and loads before attaching machinery.

Start with current-limited 12 V, motors disconnected and drives disabled.
Verify 5 V/3.3 V, USB programming, expander address/interrupt/defaults, all
limit channels, each output and E-stop. Add one unloaded motor at low current;
check current measurement, PWM transients, regeneration, ripple and heating.

Before higher-power operation, resolve the known power qualification limits:
SMCJ30A has 30 V standoff and a maximum specified pulse clamp of 48.4 V,
above DRV8701's 47 V absolute maximum; normal 32 V plus motor regeneration is
not a validated operating envelope. Q1 has meaningful I-squared-R losses
(about 1.8 W at 10 A using 18 milliohm cold resistance), the shunt return
is not a dedicated two-wire Kelvin path, bulk-capacitor ripple capability
is unverified and AP2112 dissipates about 0.51 W at 300 mA. Do not interpret
25 A fuse / 15 A chop / 100 W labels as measured continuous PCB ratings.
No hardware power redesign was introduced during this local final pass.
