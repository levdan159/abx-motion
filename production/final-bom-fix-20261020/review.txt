CORRECTIONS
The StepStick socket row incorrectly specified C145755, displayed by JLCPCB
as TC622VOA (SOIC-8 temperature sensor). This was an exported BOM/source-field
error; it was not a valid socket or a simple stock shortage. Earlier checks
verified references and geometry, but missed this part-identity error.

U501/U502 now select BOOMELE C27438, MPN 2.54-1*8P: straight, top-entry,
1x8 female socket, 2.54mm pitch, 8.5mm housing height, 3.2mm solder tails.
Four strips are needed per board: U501_L/U501_R/U502_L/U502_R.
The supplier drawing specifies 0.64 x 0.40mm tails and 1.0mm PCB holes.
All 32 socket holes were enlarged from 0.8mm to 1.0mm. Copper pads remain
1.6mm wide, leaving 0.30mm nominal annular width. Positions, pad sizes,
nets and all tracks/vias were preserved. A project-local socket footprint
and the two schematic instances/generator now agree with the physical board.
CPL row centers and rotations are byte-identical to the previous export;
these unkeyed individual socket strips are mechanically symmetric.

The BOM is consolidated by exact supplier SKU and footprint: 80 rows become
64, with all 237 placements preserved. Only the four socket placement SKUs
change. Shared connector, yellow-LED and button function-label rows now use
one catalogue-part row per SKU; green LEDs also use the catalogue MPN.
This removes duplicate-SKU rows that prompted repeated-part confirmation.
Any remaining JLCPCB warning still needs individual review. Catalogue identity
and mechanical checks are not a guarantee of current JLC assembly stock.

BEFORE ORDERING / PROTOTYPE LIMITS
Confirm U601 and connector orientation/keying, component polarity and socket
fit in the assembler's preview. Check that the four socket strips are selected;
do not approve a temperature sensor for these designators. Other unchecked
rows need their displayed warning read and reviewed before selection.

The unchanged OLED fit check remains: holes are 23.30mm apart and 23.80mm below
the header row, whereas the earlier module description specified 23.0mm.
Keep the existing spec-sheet silkscreen and rear copper logo unchanged.

Start bring-up at current-limited 12V with motors disconnected. 32V/full-current
operation remains unqualified: TVS/driver voltage coordination, Q1/LDO heating,
current-sense routing and bulk-capacitor ripple require bench verification.
Limit ports are dry-contact/3.3V only; auxiliary outputs share the 500mA PTC.
Relays and auxiliary outputs remain powered when the hardware E-stop is open.
