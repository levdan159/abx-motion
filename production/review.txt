CHANGES

20 additional 0.30 mm drilled / 0.65 mm diameter plated through-vias, explicitly
tented on both faces, located outside exposed component solder lands:
- Q1: two additional drain vias into its existing front/back V_FUSED copper.
- Q303/Q403: four additional drain vias each into the internal +BATT plane.
- Q304/Q404: two/four additional drain vias respectively.
- Q402: four additional drain vias.
Q301/Q401 already have drain-to-battery-plane vias. Q302 uses its existing
same-net via to its new backside spreader. Congested candidate positions
were rejected rather than moving or thinning any pre-existing routing.

Four bounded backside copper spreaders, each on the corresponding motor
output/drain net, sit behind Q302/Q304/Q402/Q404. These are small local areas
rather than large switching-node planes. Actual filled area is approximately
29.7 /18.6 /7.9 /33.0 mm2 respectively after clearance from existing routing.
All spreaders are connected; none remains an isolated copper island.

ESP32 U3: a grounded front-copper spreader inside the shielded module body
adds approximately32.6 mm2 of filled copper in its bounded region. Its twelve
existing plated EPAD holes and original solder/paste pattern remain intact.
The antenna/feed region and footprint keepout are untouched. No heatsink or
metal shield extension is added near the antenna.

U2 AP2112: a small grounded front-copper spreader at its ground lead adds
approximately4.5 mm2 of filled copper. Existing nearby ground vias couple it
to the planes. No fictitious exposed pad was added to this SOT-23 package.
This modest improvement does not make a 5V-to-3.3V LDO thermally unlimited.

The buck U1 retains eight thermal holes; gate drivers U301/U401 retain nine
each. D2, shunts and all component values are unchanged. No externally
accessible copper heat-transfer land was opened in the solder mask.

LIMITATIONS AND COMMISSIONING

These changes improve heat-conduction paths; they are not thermal simulation
or measured junction-temperature results. Q1 remains the likely first
high-current hotspot. The AP2112 dissipates approximately(5-3.3)*Iout and
may be stressed by sustained ESP32 activity plus external3.3V loads.
MOSFET drain copper is electrically live (+BATT, V_FUSED or motor outputs).
A shared metal heatsink or chassis contact requires an electrically insulating
thermal interface; solder mask alone is not qualified heatsink insulation.
Do not attach bare metal across the independent backside drain spreaders.

Prototype tests must include sustained Wi-Fi plus CPU activity, voltage
stability and temperature of U3/U2, followed by the previously prepared staged
motor tests and combined logic/motor operation. Check Q1, all bridge FETs,
shunts, U1/U2, inductor and capacitors. StepStick-module cooling depends on
the actual carrier fitted. Existing 7S/32V, motor compatibility, return-energy
and firmware safety limitations remain unchanged; D2 is not a braking sink.
No powered test result is claimed. All bench-results rows remain NOT RUN.
