ORIGINAL CIRCUIT PROPOSAL (antenna/placement constraints superseded as above)

SCOPE AND ARCHITECTURE
Add one non-PoE 10/100BASE-T Ethernet port in the area to the right of U3.
Use W5500 (integrated MAC and PHY) on the existing stepper SPI bus. A bare
LAN8720-style RMII PHY is insufficient for the ESP32-S3, which lacks an EMAC.
Keep motion generation local to the ESP32; networking supplies buffered commands,
configuration and telemetry. Do not promise deterministic Ethernet pulse timing
or 100 Mbit/s application throughput over the shared SPI bus.
No PoE controller, rectifier, converter or daughterboard header in this change.

MAIN PARTS
1. WIZnet W5500, LQFP-48 7x7 mm body, 0.5 mm pitch, JLC/LCSC C32843.
   JLC catalog lists this part in stock at research time; stock must be checked
   again when ordering. Device temperature range -40 to +85 C.
2. Candidate jack: genuine HanRun HR911105A, JLC/LCSC C12074, through-hole,
   integrated 10/100 magnetics and two LEDs, approximately 16.04 mm wide,
   21.3 mm deep and 13.5 mm high. Catalog lists wave-solder assembly.
   Its specified operating range is 0 to +70 C: this limits an assembly using it.
   Do not substitute an anonymous JLCPCB Assembly entry with a similar name.
   Freeze selection only after checking the manufacturer transformer diagram,
   center taps, shield pins, pin numbering, footprint and panel opening.
   If a -40 to +85 C board is required, choose a qualified industrial-temperature
   magjack before placement; do not infer compatibility from appearance.

SUPPORT CIRCUIT — DERIVE FROM THE W5500 REFERENCE, NOT A GENERIC PHY CIRCUIT
- 25 MHz crystal meeting W5500 tolerance, ESR and drive requirements, close to
  XI/XO. Compute the two load capacitors from the selected crystal CL and board
  parasitics; do not blindly copy the reference capacitor values.
- EXRES1: 12.4 kohm, 1%, with a short ground connection.
- RX/TX termination and AC-coupling network matched to the selected magjack's
  actual center-tap topology. WIZnet warns that common versus separate center
  taps require different receive circuitry. Verify the four signal polarities
  and pin mapping independently of the library symbol.
- All required AVDD/VDD bypass capacitors, local bulk capacitors, analog supply
  filtering, TOCAP (4.7uF)/1V2O (10nF) capacitors and reserved-pin treatment per
  datasheet. VBG pin 18 must float; do not add a capacitor or other load to it.
  The internal 1.2 V output is not a rail for external loads.
- Explicit PMODE pull configuration for full auto-negotiation; CS pull-up.
- Defined power-on reset plus a firmware-accessible reset. RSTn low for at least
  500 us after valid power, with startup/PLL delay respected before SPI access.
  Prefer an open-drain reset arrangement that a supervisor and firmware can
  both assert; do not connect competing push-pull outputs together.
- Link/activity LED resistors sized from the actual jack polarity and LED data.
- Ethernet-suitable low-capacitance ESD protection with a short discharge path.
  Choose PHY/cable-side placement and working voltage together with the final
  reference circuit so the transformer isolation is not bypassed.
- Separate local connector shield/chassis net. Resolve its enclosure bond and
  capacitive coupling to circuit GND deliberately; retain isolation/clearance.
  Do not simply short the shield to battery return or duplicate internal Bob
  Smith termination without inspecting the jack diagram.
- Optional source-series resistor footprints on SPI clock/data where justified
  by topology; initial stuffing and values require signal-integrity review.
- Small 3V3_ETH/GND/RESET/CS test points outside the isolation and antenna areas.

PIN PLAN — VERIFIED AGAINST THE EXISTING SCHEMATIC NETLIST
There are no unused regular output GPIOs on U3. Proposed minimal reassignment:
  Ethernet SCLK: GPIO21, existing STEP_SPI_SCLK, U3 pad 23.
  Ethernet MOSI: GPIO37, existing STEP_SPI_MOSI, U3 pad 30.
  Ethernet MISO: GPIO40, existing STEP_SPI_MISO, U3 pad 33.
  Ethernet CS_N: GPIO3, currently LED_STAT, U3 pad 15; add a pull-up.
  STAT LED: move to U601 IO1_5, pad 18; prefer active-low sinking if suitable.
  Ethernet RESET_N request: U601 IO1_6, pad 19, through the reset arrangement.
  Ethernet interrupt: initially polling; no additional ESP GPIO required.
  GPIO46: leave unused, preserving boot/download strapping behavior.

IMPORTANT: U601 pads 18 and 19 are currently connected to GND. Remove those
connections in both schematic and PCB before reusing the pins; they are not
already floating spare outputs. Preserve the existing stop/status input and
all other expander functions when changing direction/output registers.
GPIO3 is a JTAG-selection strap. Pulling it high for idle CS is consistent with
USB JTAG selection when strap selection is enabled; verify actual module/eFuse
configuration and reset behavior. No eFuse programming is part of this plan.

FIRMWARE CONTRACT
- Pin the ESP-IDF/W5500 component versions and demonstrate polling mode using
  the chosen version before committing to an interrupt-free schematic.
- All users must serialize access to the same SPI host. For stepper accesses,
  acquire the SPI bus BEFORE asserting expander-controlled CS over I2C, keep it
  acquired through the transfer, deassert CS over I2C, then release the bus.
  Transaction-level locking alone does not cover the external CS operation.
- Check every existing SPI slave releases MISO when deselected, tolerates other
  device clock modes/frequencies while deselected, and has a defined idle CS.
- Ethernet hardware CS uses the same bus arbitration. Avoid lock-order deadlocks
  involving I2C and SPI. Never perform blocking I2C transfers in ISR callbacks.
- Start Ethernet SPI at 20 MHz; qualify any higher clock on the populated board.
  Set each device's own mode and frequency. Bound Ethernet transfer sizes so
  driver configuration/status access cannot starve.
- Reset through I2C needs a board-specific callback/sequence; it is not a normal
  ESP GPIO number. Recovery must still work following brownout or ESP reboot.
- Test networking alongside all axes and fault handling; network loss must not
  bypass the existing local motion interlocks.

POWER PLAN
W5500 lists approximately 132 mA typical during 100 Mbit/s transmission at 3.3 V,
about 0.44 W. This is a typical figure, not a guaranteed maximum board budget.
Reserve at least 300 mA regulator capacity for Ethernet per the WIZnet guide,
including LED loading and margin; verify transient and temperature behavior.
Do not automatically add this load to U2, the existing AP2112K-3.3.
Preferred modest addition: a dedicated 5 V to 3.3 V regulator for 3V3_ETH,
with at least 500 mA rating and suitable thermal copper, IF the complete 5 V
budget supports it while retaining the agreed 1 A external allocation.
An LDO adds about 0.22 W heat at 132 mA; check the final regulator package and
worst-case current/ambient, not just its headline current rating.
First calculate U1's existing 2 A output budget for all simultaneous internal and
external loads. If insufficient, resolve supply capacity before freezing this
addition; do not silently reduce the external current allocation.
Keep one continuous circuit ground beneath the PHY and its digital/MDI routing;
filter the analog supply as recommended rather than splitting the ground plane.

PLACEMENT PLAN — RIGHT OF ESP32, NOT YET FIXED COORDINATES
Board envelope: x45..202 mm, y48..153 mm; U3 origin approximately (101,60) mm.
Preferred cable access is through the top edge to the right of U3. Put the
magjack at that edge, PHY behind its signal-pin side, crystal/decoupling at PHY.
Reserve roughly a 20x35 mm functional block including the jack and routing,
then expand for actual courtyards, shielding clearance, power and probe access.

Two checked constraints prevent treating the visible area as entirely empty:
1. A jack approximately x113..130, y48..70 conflicts with the existing top test
   pads and places its metal body very close to the ESP antenna. Reject this
   as a frozen placement until antenna clearance is demonstrated. Espressif
   recommends at least 15 mm antenna clearance in all directions in the product.
2. Moving the jack farther right, provisionally around x127..143, y48..70,
   improves antenna separation but overlaps the present U1 supply area.
   This is a placement-study envelope, not a verified footprint position.

Compare those envelopes using the actual antenna and connector models. Prefer
adequate antenna separation and a controlled local relocation of the entire U1
power island over placing the jack against the radiator. Preserve the converter
hot-loop geometry and feedback routing when moving the island. Check conflicts
with L1, Q1, fuse/input circuitry and the existing thermal features. Test pads can
move into accessible short rows; preserve net labels and probe clearance.
W5500 can sit below/left of the jack behind its signal pins, toward the existing
SPI connections. Check whether R747/R748/R749 need a small local relocation.
Do not spread unrelated watchdog/stepper logic or reroute the entire complex.
If this cannot fit without extensive disturbance, return the placement study
for review before changing architecture or moving the ESP32.

ROUTING AND MECHANICAL ACCEPTANCE
- Manufacturer-matched jack footprint, board-edge relationship, cable latch
  clearance, shield stakes, drill diameters and panel cutout verified in 3D.
- Preserve antenna clearance from jack metal, mounting hardware and cable plug.
- Keep TX/RX pairs short (target <25 mm), matched within each pair, nominally
  100 ohm differential using the ACTUAL fabrication stack-up. Prefer one layer,
  no pair vias, no stubs, no crossing other digital signals through the pair area.
- Preserve the PHY/pair return reference up to the isolation boundary. Apply
  WIZnet/manufacturer plane keepouts under magnetics/RJ45 and keep unrelated
  tracks out of that region on every layer. Do not extend these voids blindly
  under the PHY or SPI return paths.
- Separate Ethernet from buck switching nodes and motor-current paths.
- No long unreviewed SPI tee stubs; review the shared-bus branch topology.
- Record physical RJ45 mouth direction, pin 1 and LED polarity in the export
  orientation rules. Supplier zero-degree rotation must be verified from its
  actual model/drawing, following the recent terminal/LED export corrections.
- Additions must pass ERC, netlist/PCB parity, DRC and zero-open verification.
  Compare old/new violations and preserve all unrelated connections.

SMALL EXECUTION CHUNKS, EACH REVIEWABLE BEFORE THE NEXT
A. Freeze feasibility: connector temperature class, jack/antenna mechanical
   envelope, GPIO3 boot behavior, polling driver and full rail budget.
   Deliver a placement proposal and pin/power table; no routing.
B. Schematic: Ethernet sheet, exact support BOM and expander/LED changes;
   independent pin-by-pin reference check and ERC.
C. Placement only: magjack, PHY, crystal, reset, power, protection and test pads;
   local supply-island move only if needed. Review 2D/3D and routing corridors.
D. Route the bounded Ethernet block and reconnect locally affected nets;
   preserve other routes, then refill/check zones and DRC/parity/opens.
E. Manufacturing and bring-up: exact BOM/CPL/model orientation review, matched
   Gerbers and drills, then physical boot/reset/link/cable/traffic testing,
   concurrent motion tests, rail/thermal checks and ESD/EMC validation.
   Do not claim hardware qualification from a clean CAD check alone.
