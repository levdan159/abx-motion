ACCEPTED SCOPE AND TARGETS
- Retain 157 x105 mm outline, main floorplan, 3 DRV8462DDWR bottom-pad drivers,
  2 DC channels, CAN/RS485, I2C/OLED, UI, AUX and relay arrangement.
- 7S lithium-ion: 29.4 V full charge, 32 V normal design ceiling. A >=30 A
  battery capability does not specify the board current rating.
- Normal target: 3 axes at up to 2 A RMS/phase. Higher-current stepper-only
  target: 3 A RMS/phase, subject to qualification. Evaluate 3.5 A RMS as a
  DRIVER thermal design case, constrained by the rest of the current path.
- Keep original upright JST-XH motor connectors. Manufacturer 3 A/AWG22
  specification must be respected; a heat test alone cannot authorize a
  3.5 A port rating. A 5 A RMS/~7 A sinusoidal-peak design is a later revision.
- DC edge case: 150 W electrical input per motor for 1 second. Continuous
  current/duty and total combined load remain budget/test-derived.
- No fan. Prioritize bottom exposed pad -> solder -> GND copper/vias ->
  insulating TIM -> supported underside passive spreader/heatsink. Include
  board thermal resistance and all-axis/shared heating, not only IC theta-JC.
- Proposed qualification environment: 40 C ambient, driver junction estimate
  <=125 C including uncertainty, connector rise <=20 C and its absolute limit.
  Confirm other parts' separate derated limits; no powered tests have run.
- STEPPER_ONLY: DC outputs unloaded and commands locked off. COMBINED:
  declared DC/stepper duty. Both include Q1, regulators, ESP32/radio and I/O.
- Accepted Z addition: J607 MIN/GND/MAX with same pull-up/series/RC topology
  as existing limits, using U704 pins19/20, IO1_6/IO1_7. Shift six-connector
  bank down nominally 10.5 mm and add J607 at top, subject to actual fit.
  Existing assignments remain unchanged. New filters sit beneath the OLED.
- OLED uses actual matching direct-plug stack and 10 mm standoffs. Limit
  interfaces remain dry-contact/3V3, not direct 24 V sensor inputs. External
  STEP/DIR/EN_N interfaces are 5 V logic with defined current/cable contracts.
- Explicit arm/configuration/readback before motion, independent hardware
  E-stop/inhibit and defined fault recovery. Power off to change motor wiring.
