PROPOSED REQUIREMENTS TO FREEZE IN PHASE 1
R01 Power: conventional 7S lithium-ion, 29.4 V fully charged; 32 V normal
    design ceiling. Declare minimum operating voltage and brownout threshold
    from the selected pack's discharge limits and motor behavior. Battery
    capability of >=30 A is a source property, not a PCB current rating.
R02 Steppers: three independently configured axes, normal target up to 2 A
    RMS/phase simultaneously with specified passive heatsinks. Design and
    qualify a higher-current stepper-only profile targeting 3 A RMS per axis
    simultaneously. Evaluate 3.5 A RMS as a driver-side thermal design case;
    it is the package-class ceiling, not the installed connector/board rating.
    Set current for the actual motor; initial commissioning uses <=0.5 A RMS
    or a lower motor-rated value. Keep the user's original JST-XH connectors.
    Their manufacturer's 3 A/AWG22 rating includes contact/wire and thermal
    conditions. Qualify the actual harness at 3 A. A 3.5 A port rating requires
    an appropriate revised connector specification or manufacturer approval;
    passing a heat test alone does not override its published rating. Keep
    ordinary operation inside both the driver and connector specifications.
R03 DC: up to 150 W electrical input per motor for one second as the agreed
    intermittent edge case. Define separate continuous and repeat-duty limits
    from copper, FET, Q1, connector and thermal budgets. Include stepper,
    radio and peripheral loads in the combined input budget.
R04 Cooling: use 40 C ambient as the proposed qualification design point;
    record enclosure, orientation, heatsink/TIM geometry and natural airflow.
    Prioritize bottom exposed pad -> solder -> PCB GND/vias -> insulating
    thermal interface -> mechanically supported underside spreader/heatsink.
    Evaluate a common passive heatsink for all three axes, with thermal
    coupling included. Top-side supplementary cooling is optional. No fan.
    Propose driver junction <=125 C, with estimation uncertainty included;
    other parts remain below their own derated limits. Final limits follow
    test evidence. Do not use thermal shutdown as a normal operating boundary.
R05 Inputs: retain current dry-contact/3.3 V limit interfaces and protected
    front ends. The accepted scope includes a dedicated Z MIN/GND/MAX header
    J607 using the currently unconnected U704.19/.20 expander inputs. Reuse
    the established input
    topology, verify address/interrupt/register mapping, label and protect it.
    This provides two limits for each of the five motor channels. Shift the
    existing six-connector bank down one 10.5 mm row and add J607 in the top
    slot, subject to actual courtyard, cable, board-edge and routing checks.
    Place matching pull-up/series/RC parts below the OLED. Update both faces.
    The concept is accepted; its isolated draft is not an implemented change.
R06 External drives: three 5 V logic EN_N/STEP/DIR/GND outputs, ESP-controlled
    in EXT mode. Publish drive current, polarity, pulse timing, supported cable
    length/load and ground requirements. Arbitrary optocoupler or long-cable
    industrial interfaces require a suitable adapter and qualification.
R07 Measurements: propose VBAT accuracy +/-2% and DC driving-current readback
    +/-(5% of reading +0.1 A) after calibration over a stated range. Verify
    feasibility and specify bandwidth/filtering before advertising. DRV8701
    SO telemetry does not measure all recirculating/braking current. Stepper
    current settings/readback are distinct from measured phase RMS current.
R08 Fault behavior: motion disarmed on cold boot, MCU reset, watchdog expiry,
    failed configuration and motor fault as defined in the truth table.
    Hardware E-stop inhibits motor bridges independently of I2C/SPI/firmware;
    no automatic motion restart after clearing a fault. Declare relay/AUX
    behavior separately. Propose <=1 ms electrical E-stop-to-inhibit acceptance
    ceiling; mechanical stopping/holding is a separate mechanism-dependent
    requirement. Publish measured watchdog timeout and tolerances.
R09 Service: accessible power/control probe points, connector/pin legends,
    hardware/firmware/serial identification, configuration readback and a
    recoverable programming path. Firmware refuses incompatible hardware
    configurations. Store calibration with version/integrity information.
R10 Environmental robustness: define supported connector handling, cable
    lengths, supply transients and ESD/EMI test conditions for this lab system.
    Exact formal compliance scope is a separate product requirement; do not
    attach certification or universal peripheral compatibility claims.
R11 Operating profiles: publish independent STEPPER_ONLY and COMBINED limits.
    STEPPER_ONLY has DC outputs unloaded and commands locked off; retain
    radio, UI, logic, input-stage and peripheral heat in its budget. COMBINED
    includes both DC channels and their declared continuous/burst duty.
    Select profiles only while disarmed; firmware enforces their total-load
    budgets. Neither phase-current sums nor battery labels set input ratings.

PHASE 1 — CLOSE REQUIREMENTS AND ELECTRICAL DESIGN
Work:
- Produce one authoritative Rev1.2 connector map, GPIO/strap allocation,
  I2C addresses, SPI ownership and driver pin/MPN map from the actual schematic.
  Remove stale StepStick references from active documentation.
- Audit each DRV8462 cell against the DDWR datasheet: all supply/ground pins,
  charge-pump and DVDD capacitors, MODE/reserved pins, pull resistors,
  nSLEEP/ENABLE, CS/MISO, and configuration defaults/readback.
- Build a hardware truth table covering E-stop, warm reset, partial supplies,
  expander retained states, watchdog expiry, latched driver fault, missing
  jumper, ONBOARD/EXT mode and recovery. Quantify electrical disable timing.
  Check each DC bridge's brake versus high-impedance states and fault retry.
- Calculate combined current and heat budgets: Q1, fuse/wiring, input copper,
  bridge FETs/shunts, 5 V buck, AP2112 3.3 V LDO, ESP32 radio peaks, all new
  logic and driver VCC loads, relay coils, OLED and shared AUX allocation.
  Cooling copper is not a substitute for a failed LDO dissipation budget.
- Calculate each driver at 2, 3 and 3.5 A RMS, including hot/max FET resistance,
  switching and quiescent losses, 29.4/32 V operation and current tolerance.
  Cover holding, low-speed chopping and higher-speed motion; automatic
  standstill-current reduction is optional efficiency, not cooling credit
  for a claimed full-current operating mode.
- Model chip-to-underside resistance through solder, copper, plated vias,
  solder mask/interface and heatsink, plus mutual heating between axes.
  Junction-to-exposed-pad resistance is not junction-to-underside-heatsink
  resistance: the intervening PCB is a real part of the thermal path.
  Derive the maximum permitted local and shared heatsink resistances at 40 C
  ambient from the <=125 C driver-junction target. Include uncertainty.
- Use current 1 oz copper as the baseline. Compare 2 oz outer layers and
  additional/coordinated thermal vias where calculations justify them. Check
  fabricator fine-pitch constraints before choosing a thicker copper stackup.
- Coordinate TVS/clamp tolerances, ringing, reverse-polarity FET/gate ratings,
  component voltage limits, bulk capacitance/ripple and input fault clearing.
  Declare a bounded regenerative-energy/current envelope and the required
  battery/BMS return path or external braking arrangement. A 150 W motor
  input burst does not define returned braking energy. Do not rate D2 as
  a sustained braking sink or rely on parallel TVS current sharing.
- Freeze Z-limit circuit, pin/interrupt mapping and minimal accessible probe
  points. Define repeatable temperature-measurement locations near each
  driver, Q1, bridge, buck, LDO and ESP32; include connectors and bulk caps.
Deliverables: requirements, power/thermal calculations, complete pin map,
truth table, protection envelope, reviewed schematic and ERC dispositions.
Include one table per operating profile: per-component watts, conductor and
connector limits, assumed heatsink/interface properties and predicted margins.
Pass: no unresolved power-domain/pinout/inhibit/design-envelope blocker;
no unexplained ERC error. Warnings have exact, reviewed explanations.
Checkpoint: review these deliverables before any wider circuit change.

- Inspect assembly and unpowered shorts/polarity. Verify PowerPAD solder quality

  7S voltage; no 12 V bring-up requirement. Establish a verified return-energy
  path before any regenerative motor test; a source-only limiter is insufficient.
- Test battery-only, USB-only and dual-power/backfeed behavior. Confirm all
  rails, sequencing, boot/disarmed states and consumption before motors.
- Measure VBAT/rail ripple and droop with Wi-Fi, OLED, relays and peripherals.
  Check each I/O, Z limits, encoder, comms, mode jumper and EXT signal.
- Inspect the actual passive-heatsink fit, TIM compression and insulation,
  board support and solder-tail clearance. Establish repeatable thermocouple/

- Measure hardware E-stop latency, watchdog behavior, warm reset, brownout,
  failed I2C/SPI setup and explicit recovery using low-energy fault injection.
- Run one unloaded stepper at <=0.5 A RMS; verify coil mapping, current waveform,
  configuration readback, pulse timing and VM/phase transients. Repeat by axis.
- Test one unloaded DC channel with bounded low-power commands; calibrate

Pass: no unintended energization/restart, rails within component limits and

PHASE 7 — OPERATING-ENVELOPE QUALIFICATION
Work:
- Measure phase RMS current and winding/driver/connector heat at stepped settings
  on each axis, then all three at the proposed 2 A RMS normal target. Include
  stationary hold, slow motion and higher-speed operation with representative
  cable lengths and motors. Test enclosure and proposed 40 C ambient.
- Qualify the higher-current STEPPER_ONLY profile by progressing from the
  normal target toward 3 A RMS on one axis, then all three simultaneously.
  Use the actual AWG22/JST contact/harness construction and record its voltage
  drop and rise in temperature. Test holding and representative microstep
  positions as well as motion; retain the configured current for full-current
  thermal tests rather than relying on automatic holding-current reduction.
- Record Q1, bridges, shunts, buck, LDO, ESP32, driver, electrolytic and connector
  temperatures; measure relevant voltage drops. Estimate junction temperature
  using appropriate methods; case temperature is not junction temperature.
- Acceptance targets: no thermal warnings or faults; driver junction estimate
  including uncertainty <=125 C; other junctions below their phase-1 derated
  limits; connector rise <=20 C and absolute temperature below its specified
  maximum; all wire, capacitor, relay and supply limits respected. Thermal
  equilibrium target: <1 C drift per 10 minutes at fixed load. Do not infer
  junction temperature from case temperature plus an unrelated theta-JA.
- Progress DC tests to each 150 W/1 s, then simultaneous bursts and approved
  combined loading. Derive repetition/duty and total input limit from results.
  Test COMBINED independently; a passing STEPPER_ONLY profile does not qualify
  the same current settings while the DC channels heat the shared input stage.
- Test bounded stop/backdrive regeneration with the defined energy sink and
  battery/BMS conditions. Do not convert motor input power to assumed returned
  energy. Observe local VM and switching nodes with correctly referenced probes.
- Test fault recovery and command/link losses under bounded operating load;
  review cable/ESD/EMI susceptibility using specified lab conditions.
- Keep 3.5 A RMS as a thermal design case unless the complete current path,
  including connector specifications, permits a physical test and port rating.
  The retained 3 A JST-XH specification prevents publishing a 3.5 A port
  rating on cooling evidence alone. Leave this setting disabled if unresolved;
  a 5 A RMS / approximately 7 A sinusoidal-peak design is a later revision.
Pass: published limits supported by data, repeatable faults/stops, temperature
and waveform margins demonstrated, calibration targets met within uncertainty.
Use a repeated mixed-load run (propose 8 hours) after thermal tests pass; zero
unexplained resets/faults and retained logs. This is an acceptance screen, not
proof of lifetime/reliability statistics.
