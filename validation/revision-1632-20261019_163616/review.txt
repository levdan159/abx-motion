1. F1: verified physical pad change and supplier identity.
   Four round holes at x=160.815/174.285, y=52.3/55.7 mm, with 1.80 mm drills.
   Each vertical pair belongs to one fuse terminal. Right pair connects J1+
   V_RAW_IN; left pair connects V_FUSED. Selected SKU C352820 is Keystone
   3557-2. Manufacturer mounting dimensions are approximately 13.47 x 3.40 mm.
   The slots from the earlier direct-solder design are gone in the fresh drills.
   Remaining maintenance issue: PCB/schematic footprint identifier still says
   Fuse:Fuse_Blade_ATO_directSolder, although embedded pads are custom. Save it
   under an accurate project-library name to avoid a future library refresh
   restoring the old geometry. Exact assembly orientation still needs approval.

2. D2: the claimed 32 V protection fix is not valid.
   C310046 is Brightking SMCJ30A/TR13, with 30 V standoff and specified 48.4 V
   clamp at 31 A. DRV8701 VM absolute maximum is 47 V. Breakdown beginning
   at 33.3 V does not mean the rail is clamped at 33.3 V during a surge.
   In addition, 32 V normal input is above the 30 V standoff specification.
   Define maximum normal battery voltage, surge current/energy, temperature,
   wiring overshoot and regeneration before selecting the protection. Do not
   claim a 32 V protected design from this substitution. Lower-voltage bench
   operation is a separate, restricted use case and does not validate protection.

3. Snubber: component identity and power-budget improvement verified.
   C310/C410 are now 2.2 nF / 100 V, C107136; R310/R410 select Vishay
   CRCW080510R0FKEAHP, C844598, 10 ohm / 0.5 W. Estimated C*V^2*f loss at
   32 V / 20 kHz is 45.1 mW. This is a rating comparison, not measured thermal
   margin or proof of damping. Reducing capacitance changes damping, so tune
   from actual switching-node ringing and check pulse derating.

4. Stepper winding widths verified; Kelvin sensing remains incomplete.
   STEP1_A1/A2/B1/B2 and STEP2_A2 are 0.50 mm; STEP2_A1/B1/B2 are 0.45 mm.
   Both DRV8701 SN pins still connect via a 1.1375 mm local F.Cu ground trace
   into the driver ground region. They were not given dedicated shunt-terminal
   return connections. Widening SP does not establish Kelvin routing. Route
   separate SP and SN sense connections to the corresponding shunt terminals,
   avoiding shared motor-current copper before relying on accurate limiting.

5. Drill archive cleanup verified.
   Gerber ZIP contains separate plated/nonplated drills with no combined drill
   or drill maps. It also contains the Gerber job metadata file. Artwork/drills
   have been independently compared to this PCB revision.
