ABX-Motion V1.1 — incremental robustness revision and manufacturing review
20 October 2026

RESULT

Static checks pass. This is a prototype fabrication candidate, subject to
assembler pin-1/polarity approval. It is not a qualification of arbitrary
motors, unrestricted regenerative energy, or the printed continuous-power
targets. The intended supply is conventional 7S lithium ion (29.4 V full)
with a 32 V normal ceiling. No first-power success guarantee is possible from
CAD checks alone. The changes below address specific identified weaknesses
while preserving the completed board.

CHANGES MADE

1. Added R313/R413, 1k / 1% / 0603 / C21190, from each current-sense ADC net
   to GND. Existing R304/R404 remain 1k in series, forming 1:2 dividers.
   A nominal 4.8 V amplifier output now yields 2.4 V at the ESP32 rather than
   4.8 V. Even a conservative assumed 5.5 V SO bound gives 2.778 V with
   worst-case 1% divider tolerances. The nominal ADC RC time constant is now
   approximately 5 us. Hardware current-chopping references are unchanged.
   R313 is at (171,90) mm; R413 is at (156.5,101.5) mm. New routes are short
   branches into existing current-sense vias, with local ground-plane vias.

2. R2 changed 100k -> 150k / C22807, retaining its footprint and every track.
   R3 remains 10k: ADC voltage is now nominally VBAT/16. At 32 V it is 2.0 V;
   at a 40 V clamped pulse it is 2.5 V. Worst-case 1% resistors give 2.993 V
   even at 47 V. That calculation is input-divider margin, not permission
   to operate the drivers at their 47 V absolute maximum.

3. R6 changed 10k -> 22k / C31850 in the same footprint. At 32 V and a
   nominal 2.1 V LED, dissipation falls from about 89 mW to 41 mW. LED current
   becomes approximately 1.36 mA; brightness will be lower.

4. D2 changed from SMCJ30A to TI TVS3300DRVR / C2863624, at the same
   (182,63.5) mm center. The new local footprint follows TI's DRV0006A land
   pattern: six 0.45 x 0.30 mm lands, 0.65 mm pitch, 1.95 mm column centers,
   1.0 x 1.6 mm ground exposed pad. GND is pins 1/2/3/EP7; IN is pins 4/5/6.
   All six leads are connected, with short local power/ground fanout and
   three extra ground vias outside the solder lands. There are no new holes
   in its exposed solder pad. The manufacturer datasheet is archived here.
   A project-local symbol and matching generator definitions retain the
   actual pin numbers. One existing spec-table string now says TVS3300.

   The device has 33 V standoff and a maximum 40 V clamp under TI's specified
   8/20 us test conditions, providing voltage headroom below DRV8701's
   45 V recommended and 47 V absolute maxima. Board parasitic overshoot must
   still be measured. This is pulse protection, not a sustained bus regulator.

IMPORTANT PROTECTION TRADEOFF / SYSTEM LIMIT

The new clamp trades the SMCJ package's greater long-pulse energy capability
for tighter voltage clamping. Its SON rating is 35 A for 8/20 us, but ONLY
3.5 A for the specified 10/1000 us waveform; DC breakdown current is limited
to 10 mA. Those values must not be confused with a 30 A continuous rating.
The former SMCJ30A was not guaranteed to protect the drivers' voltage limit.
The new part fixes voltage coordination for its rated short-surge envelope,
not an unknown motor braking-energy budget. It must not be used to absorb
sustained motor regeneration or to clear a battery overvoltage through F1.

Different peripherals are supported within electrical limits, not by assuming
all driver carriers and mechanical loads are interchangeable. A regenerative
load needs a battery/BMS capable of accepting the charging current, or a
separately sized braking-energy sink that operates before damaging bus rise.
Full-charge or BMS-disconnected braking needs particular attention. If the
application requires the PCB alone to survive such cases with unspecified
loads, this revision is NOT a complete solution; a specified braking/clamp
design is still required. No onboard braking infrastructure was added.

ASSEMBLER ACTION BEFORE ORDERING

Supplier CAD access for the new clamp returned HTTP 403. Accordingly, do NOT
treat its provisional CPL angle of 180 degrees as supplier-pad verified.
The authoritative required placement is: viewed from the TOP of the PCB with
the XT60 at the upper-right, D2 physical pin 1 is at the LOWER-RIGHT corner,
(182.975,64.150) mm. The three LEFT leads (4/5/6) are +BATT; the three RIGHT
leads (1/2/3) and center exposed pad are GND. See assembly-D2-pinmap.svg.
Confirm the actual package pin-1 mark in JLC's placement editor and correct
the editor rotation if necessary before approving assembly. Do not approve
an old SMC diode or the former D2 footprint. Also retain U601 pin-1/keying
checks and the usual XT60, diode and electrolytic polarity checks from the
previous review. Current live JLC assembly stock/order state is not accessible
to this local review. Catalogue identity does not establish stock or rotation.

FIRMWARE / FIRST POWER

Use VBAT = 16*Vadc nominally, calibrated for ADC and resistor errors. Use
Ishunt = (2*Vadc - calibrated_SO_offset)/(20*0.010), with measured gain/offset
calibration and appropriate ADC sampling/filtering. Sampling SO in recirculating
brake mode does not measure winding current. No application firmware was
created or claimed tested in this hardware pass.

Before connecting a high-current battery without instrumentation, validate
initial power at the intended voltage with controlled fault energy/current,
motors and plug-in carriers disconnected. A 12 V bring-up is not required.
Check rails, USB-only and dual-source behavior, sustained radio/LDO stability,
expander cold/warm-reset defaults, all limits, outputs and E-stop. Then test
one compatible unloaded motor/carrier at reduced current, observing bus
overshoot, regenerative voltage and temperatures before adding load.

Q1 conduction losses, LDO dissipation, capacitor ripple rating and shunt Kelvin
accuracy remain hardware-qualification limits. The pack's >=30 A capability
does not make this PCB a 30 A continuous controller. The 25 A fuse / nominal
15 A chopping / 100 W printed targets are not measured board ratings.
DRV8825 is the carrier pinout basis; TMC2209's 29 V operating maximum does
not cover 7S full charge or the 32 V target. Confirm actual OLED mounting:
23.30 mm board hole spacing / 23.80 mm below its header remains unchanged.
Hardware E-stop gates drivers, but does not remove relay/AUX power. Limits
remain dry-contact/3.3 V only. Preserve grounded PCA bits as inputs and
initialize output latches on every boot, including warm MCU-only resets.
