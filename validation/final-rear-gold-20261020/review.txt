VERDICT

The completed compact 157 x 105 mm layout passes all independent electrical,
DRC, schematic-parity and Gerber-isolation checks after the local rear-copper
branding and spec-table polish pass. It is ready for controlled, low-current
12 V prototype bring-up. This is not an unconditional production or 32 V /
full-current qualification. Before ordering assembly, confirm supplier
placement/polarity in the assembler's DFM preview (especially U601, J601-J606,
and the corrected XT60/LED orientations) and verify physical OLED mounting
hole alignment against the chosen display module.

ASSEMBLY, MECHANICAL AND ELECTRICAL CAVEATS

- Supplier CPL & Pin-1 Verification: U601 (PCA9555PW,118, C128392, CPL 270 deg)
  and J601-J606 (B3B-PH-K-S, C131339, CPL 180 deg) CPL angles were not
  independently pad-verified against live supplier CAD due to HTTP 403 on those
  endpoints. Confirm U601 pin 1 (upper-left on PCB), J601-J606 pin 1 (MIN/+5V_OUT
  end) and housing key orientation, D2/electrolytic/LED polarity, Q1 orientation
  and J1 XT60-M (C98733) +/- polarity in the assembler's placement preview.
- OLED Mounting Holes (H5/H6): Located at (67.85, 105.30) and (91.15, 105.30) mm
  (23.30 mm horizontal spacing, 23.80 mm below J208 at Y=81.50 mm) versus the
  23.0 mm nominal silkscreen callout. Verify mechanical hole pattern against the
  actual 0.96"/1.3" OLED module before ordering.
- Compact I/O Limits: J601-J604 accept dry contacts to GND or 3.3 V logic ONLY
  (do not connect 12/24 V sensors). J605-J606 share F5 (1206L050/15YR, 500 mA
  hold at 20 C); thermal PTC protection derates with ambient temperature and is
  not an independent per-channel current regulator.
- Firmware & Safety: Initialize U601 on every boot (keep ports as inputs, write
  Port 1 output latch 0x0C, then set Port 1 direction 0xF0 and Port 0 direction
  0xFF; keep grounded Port 1 bits 5..7 as inputs). Hardware E-stop (J102) gates
  DC bridges and stepper enables, while relays and auxiliary outputs remain
  powered. I2C limit sensing is not a hardware overtravel interlock.
- Power Envelope: SMCJ30A's 48.4 V maximum surge clamp exceeds DRV8701E's 47 V
  absolute maximum, so 32 V operation is not qualified. Begin bring-up at 12 V
  with current limiting, unloaded motors, and thermal/transient measurements.
