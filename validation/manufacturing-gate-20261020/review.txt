ABX-Motion V1.1 — final manufacturing gate
20 October 2026

DECISION: HOLD FOR THE REQUESTED DIRECT 7S BATTERY / 32 V ENVELOPE

The fabrication data is internally consistent and the routing is complete.
However, the present design has not established the protection margins needed
for direct battery-powered motor operation at this voltage. Resolve the two
electrical items below before approving this revision for that use. This is
a focused power/sensing correction, not a request to expand the I/O architecture.

Operating target supplied by the user: 7S lithium ion, assuming conventional
4.2 V/cell charging (29.4 V full), with support/margin through 32 V. Battery
continuous current capability is at least 30 A. That does NOT mean the board
will draw 30 A or is rated to carry 30 A; actual motor loads remain unspecified.
Earlier reports recommending controlled 12 V testing do not approve this new
use case. No electrical source, routing or fabrication/upload file was changed
by this review. The production status pointer is marked HOLD separately.

1. BUS PROTECTION NEEDS A GUARANTEED VOLTAGE/ENERGY BUDGET

D2 is Brightking SMCJ30A/TR13, LCSC C310046. Its 30 V reverse standoff is
below the requested 32 V normal ceiling. The series table specifies breakdown
33.3–36.8 V and maximum clamp 48.4 V at 31 A, for the rated pulse conditions.
DRV8701 VM has a 45 V recommended maximum and 47 V absolute maximum.
Consequently, the present component selection does not guarantee protection
of the motor drivers across the requested voltage and transient envelope.
The specified clamp is not the voltage that every small transient will reach;
actual clamp depends on current, temperature and wiring inductance. Those
bounds have not been established here. Absolute maximum is not a design target.

At 29.4 V the TVS is below its standoff rating, so this is not a claim that
connecting a charged 7S pack necessarily causes immediate failure. It is a
missing guaranteed margin for hot-plug, switching and regenerative conditions.
A fully charged pack or a BMS that rejects/disconnects charging may not absorb
regenerated motor energy. A TVS pulse rating does not establish a continuous
braking-energy rating.

Required resolution: select a protection arrangement whose guaranteed normal
voltage rating covers 32 V and whose worst-case clamp, overshoot and energy
remain within the limits of every attached device. Establish transient current
and braking energy from the actual motors, wiring and battery/BMS behavior.
This may be a qualified clamp/filter solution, or a bus braking clamp if the
load requires one. Do not blindly install a higher-voltage SMCJ: that raises
the clamp voltage. Lower SMCJ26A/28A parts would compromise normal 7S voltage.
Input overvoltage disconnection alone does not absorb energy generated on the
motor side. If a compact protection solution cannot meet the budget, change
the driver/rating or explicitly narrow the operating envelope before release.

2. PROTECT THE THREE ESP32 ANALOG INPUTS

Verified against the newly exported netlist:
  U301 SO -> R304 1k -> M1_CS / GPIO6, with C305 10nF to ground.
  U401 SO -> R404 1k -> M2_CS / GPIO8, with C405 10nF to ground.
There is no divider or external clamp on either path. DRV8701 SO is a gain-20
shunt-amplifier output; its transfer characteristic extends toward AVDD,
nominally 4.8 V. The approximately 3 V current-chop reference is not an SO
voltage clamp. Normal regulated current can keep SO within range, but fault,
blanking and transient excursions are not guaranteed to do so. The RC filter
and 1k series resistor do not establish an allowed continuous overvoltage or
injection-current rating at the ESP32. Espressif specifies 3.6 V GPIO tolerance.

Minimal local correction to evaluate: add a 1k shunt resistor to ground on
each ADC side of R304/R404, forming 1:2 dividers. A 4.8 V SO then yields
2.4 V nominal at the ADC; worst-case AVDD, resistor tolerance, ADC source
impedance and power sequencing still need checking. Each SO load is 2k,
approximately 2.4 mA at 4.8 V, below TI's stated 5 mA source capability.
The existing capacitor sees about 500 ohm Thevenin resistance instead of
1k. Update firmware scaling and sampling calibration accordingly. Additional
clamps, if used, need a defined current path and powered-off behavior.

VBAT_SENSE / GPIO1 uses R2=100k above R3=10k and C1=100nF to ground:
  29.4 V bus -> 2.673 V ADC; 32 V -> 2.909 V: normal voltages are acceptable.
  48.4 V bus -> 4.400 V ADC: present pulse-clamp ceiling is not acceptable.
An approximately 1:16 divider (for example R2=150k, R3=10k) would reduce
48.4 V to 3.025 V nominal and retain useful battery measurement resolution.
Choose its final ratio against the RESOLVED bus limit and tolerance; account
for ADC range, filtering and unpowered input behavior. This is a proposed
correction, not an applied or hardware-tested revision.

ADDITIONAL ITEMS TO CLOSE WITHOUT REBUILDING THE BOARD

- Choose the actual StepStick carrier before power approval. The schematic
  basis is DRV8825; check carrier capacitor voltage, pinout, enable polarity
  and supply transient limit. TMC2209 is not a default 7S/32 V substitute:
  its published operating supply maximum is 29 V, below 29.4 V full charge.
- F1 C352820 is the Keystone 3557-2 HOLDER. A physical blade fuse is a separate
  item and is not represented by another blade-fuse BOM row. Obtain a fuse
  with suitable DC voltage, interruption and current ratings; do not bridge
  the holder for battery testing. A 25 A fuse label is not a PCB current rating.
- R6 remains 10k in a 100mW 0603 part. At 32 V with a 2.1 V LED its nominal
  dissipation is about 89mW. A 22k value in the same footprint is a simple
  improvement in thermal margin, subject to acceptable LED brightness.
- At actual intended motor load, qualify Q1 and the power paths thermally.
  Q1 cold losses at 18 milliohm are 1.8 W at 10 A and 4.05 W at 15 A; hot
  resistance increases loss. The battery's available 30 A does not establish
  this operating current. Driver chop current and fuse rating do not certify
  continuous board current. Motor, relay and connector limits apply separately.
- AP2112 dissipates approximately 0.51 W at 300mA / 0.85 W at 500mA from 5 V.
  Test sustained radio activity and external 3.3 V loading for heat/brownouts.
- Bulk-capacitor ripple/ESR and current-sense accuracy still need load tests.
  SN reaches local ground rather than a dedicated two-wire Kelvin return to
  its shunt. Do not treat the nominal current threshold as measured accuracy.

ASSEMBLY / FIT / FIRMWARE LIMITS

Keep the present front branding and rear copper gears/white text. No aesthetic
edit is needed for this manufacturing gate. Keep the spec-sheet wording as
the user requested. Six I/O connectors mean four dual-limit connectors
(eight inputs) plus two switched-output connectors, not six input channels.
Limits are dry-contact/3.3 V only; AUX outputs share a thermal 500mA PTC.

Supplier pad rotation conventions for U601 and the new PH connectors were
not independently confirmed by live supplier CAD (earlier endpoint access
failed). Approve actual pin 1/keying against PCB pads in JLC's placement
editor; component presence or a plausible body outline is insufficient.
Review polarized electrolytics, D2, Q1 and XT60 markings against their nets.
The corrected socket row must be C27438, never the former TC622VOA sensor.
Through-hole assembly process and the open exposed-pad thermal holes need
assembler acceptance. Actual OLED mounting still requires checking the module:
PCB hole spacing is 23.30 mm, 23.80 mm below its header, different from an
earlier 23.0 mm module assumption. No missing-hole conclusion follows from
the 3D connector render alone.

Hardware E-stop gates brushed-driver sleep and onboard/external stepper
enable, but relay coils and AUX outputs remain powered. External driver
electrical compatibility is required. Limits are firmware/I2C sensed; they
are not a separate hardware overtravel stop. Firmware must block motion
further into an active limit, permit controlled retreat, initialize expander
latches before enabling outputs, and handle warm ESP32 resets where the
expander retains state. Grounded unused PCA9555 pins must remain inputs.
No complete, tested application firmware has been established by this review.

RELEASE PATH

Make the small sensing/LED corrections, resolve the bus-protection budget
for the actual drivers and battery/BMS, then regenerate a new matching upload
set and repeat ERC/DRC/parity/package checks. Do not enlarge the board or add
general industrial I/O as part of these fixes. Validate rails/programming at
the intended voltage with motors disconnected and suitably limited available
fault energy, then add one unloaded motor at reduced current. Measure bus
overshoot/regeneration and temperatures before approving load operation.
The request to use a battery does not substitute for a first-power protection
plan. Tests can be performed at 29.4 V without requiring a 12 V operating mode.
