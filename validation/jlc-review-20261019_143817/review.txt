PLACEMENT AND ROTATION REVIEW
The supplied screenshot suggests U1 and D2 are rotated across their board pad directions, and U3 is displaced. These visual observations require model/pin matching before applying numerical corrections.
The current CPL already applies many per-footprint rotations and centre shifts: U1/U301/Q301/D2=270deg, C306=180deg, J102=270deg, JST left-edge connectors=90deg, bottom JST connectors=180deg, relay=270deg.
Some such corrections may be correct for a particular JLC library model. Do not apply a global angle reset or copy the same offset across all parts in a package family.
For each part, compare supplier pin geometry, PCB pad geometry, polarity and connector facing. Then encode verified offsets keyed to the actual supplier part, and re-upload the same BOM/CPL pair.
JLC requires component centroid X/Y and counterclockwise-positive rotation; these requirements alone do not establish the zero orientation or model origin for each particular part.
JLC reference: https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/pick-place-file-for-pcb-assembly
JLC orientation reference: https://jlcpcb.com/blog/files-needed-for-pcb-assembly
