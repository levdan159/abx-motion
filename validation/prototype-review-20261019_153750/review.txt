ABX-Motion first-prototype review — 19 October 2026

Decision
The architecture is suitable for a controlled, low-power first prototype after
the fuse-holder footprint and assembly alignment are resolved. Do not release
the present package for assembly solely on the strength of its 3D preview.
The advertised 32 V / 15 A per-channel / 25 A input capability is not validated.
No schematic, PCB, BOM, CPL, or production ZIP was modified by this review.

Scope and evidence
Reviewed the saved files identified by manifest.json, with KiCad 10.0.7.
The source hashes were unchanged when the user's latest assembly image arrived.
That image can reflect changes in JLC's placement editor without a PCB revision.
Fresh ERC: zero violations across six hierarchical sheet instances.
Fresh DRC with zone refill and schematic parity: zero errors, zero unconnected
items, zero parity discrepancies, 22 warnings. These are 12 library footprint
differences, nine silk/edge warnings, and one mirrored reference warning.
A separate snapshot check imposing 0.15 mm minimum track width and clearance
also returned zero errors; adding 0.15 mm silk clearance produced 32 warnings.
The saved project itself still has zero global minimum width/clearance, with
0.15 mm default net clearance. Some checks are disabled; see raw reports.
Component values and footprint names match between schematic and PCB.
All four copper layers, both masks, both paste layers, both silkscreens, outline,
and separate PTH/NPTH drill files match regenerated exports, excluding metadata.
The 1537 BOM has 61 supplier SKUs and 190 placements; CPL has the same 190 refs,
with no duplicate designators. Supplier identity checks are recorded separately.
Catalogue identity does not establish every land pattern, orientation, or stock.

Confirmed strengths
- Continuous In1 ground plane, with no signal tracks on that layer.
- Useful power/current test points, accessible connectors and motor sections.
- Correct ESP32-S3-WROOM-1-N8R2 supplier identity, avoiding the previous variant
  conflict with GPIO35/36/37 used by this design.
- Correct DRV8701E PH/EN pin assignment, charge-pump capacitor connections, and
  200 kohm IDRIVE resistors. Nominal current chopping is approximately 14.75 A
  using 4.8 V AVDD, the 18k/30k divider, 20 V/V gain, 50 mV offset and 10 mohm.
- Selected shunts are now CRA2512-FZ-R010ELF, 10 mohm / 3 W.
- Motor bulk capacitors C306/C406 now select 220 uF / 50 V / 10 mm parts;
  C501/C502 select 100 uF / 50 V / 8 mm parts. Snubber capacitors are 100 V.
- The RS485 transceiver, relay and tactile-switch catalogue identities are
  corrected relative to earlier erroneous BOM revisions.

Resolve before ordering
1. F1 mechanical incompatibility. BOM C207061 is Littelfuse 178.6165.0002,
   with four-pin terminals. PCB still uses Fuse_Blade_ATO_directSolder, with
   two 6 x 2 mm slots on 9.3 mm centres. These are not the holder land pattern.
   The PCB also retains the old, incorrect C2897423 supplier field for F1.
   Use the exact holder drawing to replace the footprint, route it, update the
   schematic and supplier field, and regenerate Gerbers/BOM/CPL. A holder
   appearing in JLC's preview does not change the drilled holes.
2. Complete placement approval against real pad/pin identities. In particular
   verify U1, Q1, D1/D2, U3 and polarized capacitors. Supplier angle conventions
   vary, so differences from KiCad angles alone do not prove an error.
   The four socket CPL centres ARE correct: U501_L (100.5,-124.39), U501_R
   (113.2,-124.39), U502_L (121,-124.39), U502_R (133.7,-124.39), in CPL mm.
   Their centres match PCB hole-row means. Earlier screenshot-only diagnosis
   of incorrect socket coordinates was not supported by the actual file.
3. Clean the manufacturing archive. It contains both combined MixedPlating
   drills and separate PTH/NPTH drills, plus drill maps. Submit one consistent
   drill set; separate PTH/NPTH is the clearer choice. The matching copper
   export is a positive result, but redundant drill inputs invite CAM confusion.

Resolve before higher-voltage or higher-current tests
4. D2 is SMCJ28A: 28 V standoff, 31.1–34.4 V breakdown, and 45.4 V maximum
   clamp at its specified pulse current and temperature. This does not provide
   comfortable coordination between a normal 32 V rail and DRV8701's 47 V
   absolute maximum. Account for ringing, temperature and motor regeneration.
   Do not simply increase the TVS voltage without rechecking downstream limits.
5. Q1 is a thermal bottleneck. At 18 mohm, calculated cold conduction losses
   are 0.45 W at 5 A, 1.8 W at 10 A, 4.05 W at 15 A and 11.25 W at 25 A.
   Resistance rises with temperature. Datasheet case-cooled current/power
   ratings do not certify this PCB. Measure temperature and voltage drop.
6. Shunt sensing is not fully Kelvin routed. Each DRV SN pin goes to local
   ground, rather than a dedicated connection to its shunt ground terminal;
   SP is picked up through the bridge-source routing. Route both sense leads
   separately to the shunt terminals before relying on current accuracy at load.
7. R310/R410 are 10 ohm, 125 mW 0805 snubber resistors. For full rail swings,
   approximate RC loss C*V^2*f with 10 nF at 20 kHz is 29 mW at 12 V,
   115 mW at 24 V, and 205 mW at 32 V. Actual loss depends on switching.
   Use adequate continuous and pulse ratings, then tune from measured ringing.
8. StepStick winding routes are 0.25 mm on nominal 35 um copper. Individual
   paths are roughly 22–37 mm, with calculated cold resistance 44–73 mohm.
   At 2 A each path dissipates approximately 0.18–0.29 W. Widen them before
   claiming robust 2 A operation. Relay contact routes are 0.8 mm; the relay's
   nameplate rating likewise does not establish PCB current capability.
9. AP2112 dissipates approximately (5-3.3)*I: 0.51 W at 300 mA and 0.85 W at
   500 mA. Check sustained ESP32 radio activity and external 3.3 V loads for
   temperature and brownouts. A switching 3.3 V supply would improve margin.
10. Bulk capacitor voltage ratings are corrected, but ripple-current and ESR
    suitability remain unproven. The selected 220 uF catalogue part lists
    230 mA ripple at 120 Hz, not a validated multiamp motor PWM capability.
    Include ceramic contribution, battery wiring and switching frequency in
    the assessment; observe bus ripple and capacitor temperature on the bench.

Functional limits to test explicitly
The E-stop loop controls brushed-driver sleep and onboard StepStick sleep/reset.
External STEP_ENABLE remains an MCU-controlled net independent of that loop;
relay outputs also remain powered. Verify the actual stop behavior of every
connected driver before testing machinery. Do not infer a universal stop function.
The DRV8825 carrier pinout is the design basis; other StepStick modules require
individual compatibility checks. OLED hole spacing is 23.3 mm horizontally with
the lower row 23.8 mm from the header; confirm against the purchased module.
Current-sense outputs and VBAT sense deserve transient/clamp checks at the ESP32
ADC pins. USB supplies +5 V through D202; test USB-only and dual-source behavior,
including possible reverse transfer through the FPWM buck to its input rail.
R6's battery LED resistor is 10k / 100 mW: approximately 89 mW at 32 V with a
2.1 V LED, leaving little hot-ambient margin. Increase resistance or power rating.
Open thermal holes in exposed pads require an assembly-process review for solder
wicking; the library mismatch warnings mostly reflect increased thermal drills.

Suggested first bring-up
A. Inspect actual assembly against pin 1 and diode/capacitor polarity. Check
   resistance between each supply rail and ground before applying power.
B. Leave motors, StepStick modules, external loads and encoders disconnected.
   Keep the E-stop loop open. Apply 12 V from a current-limited bench supply;
   use a low limit for initial short detection, then enough for MCU startup.
   Measure +5 V and +3.3 V, supply current and regulator temperatures.
C. Flash firmware with motor/relay outputs inactive. Exercise reset, boot,
   buttons, OLED, CAN, RS485 and encoders. Run sustained radio activity while
   monitoring +3.3 V and AP2112 temperature. Verify USB power-source behavior.
D. Test one unloaded motor at a time with a deliberately reduced hardware
   current limit. Replacing each 30k VREF lower divider resistor with 1.8k
   would give approximately 1.9 A nominal chopping under the assumptions above;
   calculate and measure the actual limit before attaching a motor. A bench
   supply limit alone does not bound recirculating winding or capacitor current.
   Start StepStick winding current conservatively, around 0.5 A, and test relay
   outputs with small low-voltage loads.
E. Observe VM overshoot/ripple, gate waveforms, current-sense accuracy, Q1,
   shunts, bridge FETs and capacitor temperature before increasing load.
   Bench supplies often cannot absorb regeneration; provide a suitable energy
   sink for deceleration tests. Verify stop behavior before mechanical loading.
