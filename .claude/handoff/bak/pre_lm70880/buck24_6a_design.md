# +24 V aux rail, 6 A continuous / 8 A capable: design (2026-10-24, research only, nothing edited)

Inputs checked:
- The board: U511 TPS54560B block on the **Brakes** sheet. It is generated by `bl/aux.py`, which writes `Brakes.kicad_sch`, not Power.
- Input TVS: D2 **SMCJ43A** on +BATT, behind F1 (10 A ATO) and Q1. VBR is 47.8-52.8 V; VC is **69.4 V at 21.7 A**. The bus breaker opens at up to 150 A, so a battery-lead kick can reach this clamp.
- Stack-up: outer layers 2 oz, inner 1 oz. In1 = GND, 0.21 mm under F.Cu.
- JLC data: the cdfer in-stock snapshot dated 2026-10-24 (local DB). Stock and prices are at qty 1.

**Budget note:** +24V also feeds the brake output through D523. The DRV8874 runs about 1.5 A continuous and trips at 3 A. So 6 A for v1.2 means **7.5 A or more on the rail**. An 8 A part is needed, not just nice to have.

**Existing-part flag:** JLC lists L520 MWSA1265S-220MT (C7245133) at **Isat 7.5 A, Irms 6 A**. aux_design.md assumed 9.5 A or more. The present inductor saturates near the TPS54560B current limit.

**L520 and the inductor current**
- Eq 7 (slope compensation): L(sc) = 24 × 5 / (24 × 0.5) = 10 µH. At 400 kHz the minimum would be 12.5 µH, and 15 µH parts are 19-28 mΩ; that is why fsw is 500 kHz.
- Ripple: 1.6 A at 36 V, 2.06 A at 42 V, 2.4 A at 48 V.
- Peak current at 8 A and 42 V: 9.0 A, which is under the 10.0 A minimum limit.
- Short-circuit peak (Eq 34): 12.4 + 48 V × 75 ns / 10 µH = 12.8 A, which is under the 15.5 A Isat.
- Footprint: check the pads against `L_Sunlord_MWSA1265S` (3.25×5.5 mm at ±5.625 mm).
- Fallback: MWSA1265S-100MT (C7009501) drops onto the existing footprint, but it is 16.5 mΩ with Isat 12 A, so it is good for 6 A only.

**Input caps (C520-C523)**
- RMS current: 4 A at 8 A, D = 0.5 (Eq 38).
- Effective capacitance at 36 V is about 9.4 µF, so ripple is about 0.38 V p-p at 8 A (Eq 16).

**Output caps (C529-C533)**
- About 22.5 µF effective at 24 V.
- Load-off step of 8 A with 5 % overshoot needs 10.8 µF (Eq 13).
- Ripple: about 25 mV.
- RMS current: 0.6 A.

**BIAS and PFM tie-offs**
- BIAS to GND costs about 0.15-0.3 W of VCC LDO loss. In return it is immune to v1.2 back-feed pushing +24V toward BIAS's 32 V absolute maximum. Tie BIAS to +24V only if v1.2 can never regenerate.
- FPWM gives a fixed frequency and lets the buck sink back-fed current, up to about 6 A (30 mV / RS).

**Dropout (Eq 1)**
- Off-time skipping starts below 24 × 2.0/(2.0 − 0.126) = **25.6 V** (25.1 V typical).
- Below that, duty extends to about 99 %, so +24V regulates down to about 24.5 V in and tracks the input below that.
- This is better than the TPS54560B, which drops out at 25.4 V with no tracking.

**Soft-start:** fixed internally at 2.8 ms (1.9-4.4 ms). The charge current into v1.2's bulk is C × 8.6 V/ms. Keep v1.2's input bulk at or below about 470 µF (about 4 A), or hold its loads off until PG. Hiccup is disabled during soft-start, so a larger bulk only rides the current limit.

### Overload: v1.2 drawing 16 A for 1 s
- **Cycle-by-cycle peak limit:** 10.0/11.2/12.4 A. That gives about **9.2/10.4/11.6 A average out** at 36 V.
- **No collapse while Vout stays at or above 12 V:**
  - Hiccup needs FB < 0.4 V (Vout < 12 V) for 512 consecutive limited cycles.
  - Until then the counter restarts, and the rail sags to I_lim × R_load, about 15-16 V for a 1.5 Ω stalled motor.
  - It recovers as the motor's back-EMF rises.
  - PG drops below 22.1 V.
- **Hiccup when the sag goes deeper (Vout < 12 V):**
  - Off after 1 ms, then 16384 cycles (33 ms), then a 2.8 ms soft-start, repeating.
  - At this point the motor never starts, and v1.2's logic resets.
- **Thermal:** one second at about 10.5 A is about 6 W in the IC, a transient rise of tens of °C. TSD is at 175 °C and is not expected.
- **Conclusion:**
  - **No 8 A buck delivers 16 A for 1 s.** That is 384 W; capacitors cannot bridge 16 C.
  - v1.2 must limit DC-motor inrush to 6 A or less (driver ITRIP or a PWM ramp), or take its DC motors from the battery.
  - If 16 A is a hard requirement: dual-phase LM70880 (CONFIG 54.9 k primary / 90.9 k secondary, external comp, about +350 mm²), with a 16 A rating and a 20-22 A limit.

### 8 A continuous
- **Electrically:** yes, within the limit (9.0 A peak against 10.0 A minimum).
- **Thermally:** about 3.5-4 W in the IC.
  - TI's EVM RθJA is 18.6 °C/W. On this tight board 22-25 °C/W is more realistic.
  - That is a rise of about 80-95 °C, or TJ ≈ 125-140 °C at 45 °C ambient.
- **Rating:** **6 A continuous, 8 A for minutes or with airflow.** Use the full EP via field to In1 GND, and put VIN-pad vias to the In2 +BATT plane.
- **Fuse:** at 8 A out from a 25 V pack, the input is about 8 A plus the logic branch. Change **F1 to 15 A** if 8 A continuous is wanted; 10 A is fine for 6 A.
- **+24V path to J510:** check the In2 +24V route from L520 to J510, about 40 mm on 1 oz copper. It needs about 7-8 mm of width for 8 A.

**Area:** about the same as the U511 block today (±20 mm²):
- The IC is 49 against 45 mm².
- D520 goes (−36 mm²).
- RS adds 10 mm², and the four or five extra 0603/0805 parts add about 32 mm².
