## 1. Datasheet re-check of `buck24_6a_design.md` (what was right, what changed)
Confirmed from SNVSCD3: pin map (table 4-1), Eq 3 RT (43.2k -> 500.7 kHz), Eq 5 FB (348k/12k -> 24.0 V),
Eq 7 slope (10 uH = 1x at 5 mOhm, 500 kHz), Eq 8 RS (56 mV / 5 mOhm; 5 mOhm is the LM70880 minimum), current limit
50/56/62 mV, Eq 2 UVLO (267k/10k: 27.7 V on, 90 % = 24.9 V off), Eq 42-44 compensation (4.42k / 15 nF / 100 pF:
fc 25 kHz, zero 2.4 kHz, pole 261 kHz with CBW 38 pF), CBOOT 47 nF **plus 1 Ohm in series** (required when VCC = 8 V,
i.e. adjustable output), VCC 4.7 uF to PGND, VDDA 100 nF to AGND, CONFIG 41.2k = standalone + spread spectrum,
PFM/SYNCIN to AGND = FPWM, BIAS "tie to AGND if unused" (allowed for Vout <= 30 V too), EN rated 80 V (87.5 abs),
hiccup 512 / 16384 cycles, soft start 2.8 ms (1.9-4.4), TSD 175 C.

Corrections and additions:
1. **Die pads.** VIN die pad = pins 1, 2, 27, 28, 29 merged (pin 3 is a separate pad); SW die pad = pins 20-22 merged;
   PGND die pad 30 sits bottom-centre (3.125 x 1.584 mm, centred 0.037 mm right of the package centre). The top-right
   die pad is SW, not PGND: the PGND *pins* 23-26 are on the top edge right of the VIN fingers, so the input caps still
   bridge VIN -> PGND above the IC as the doc said.
2. **Pin 12 is offset 0.037 mm**: pads 12 (VOUT, +24V = Power_HV 0.25 mm) and 13 (CONFIG) are only 0.213 mm apart.
   The footprint gives both a 0.2 mm local clearance (DRC clean), and the VOUT track leaves pin 12 at 0.15 mm width.
3. **Hiccup detail**: the 512-cycle counter is reset by four consecutive cycles without a current-limit hit; the SS
   clamp (150 mV above FB) needs 16 limit events.
4. **Dropout**: Eq 1 uses the typical 88 ns off-time: 24 x 2 us / (2 us - 88 ns) = 25.1 V (the doc's 25.6 V was the
   126 ns maximum; both quoted now).
5. **Shunt**: TI's reference designs use a wide-terminal 0508 (Susumu KRL3216, lower ESL); kept the in-stock 1206
   RLM12FTCMR005 (0.32 W at 8 A). It now sits in C529's old place (no free spot "beside L520 pad 2"); C529 moved
   below C531.
6. **PG pull-up R541 dropped**: no +3V3 copper nearby and no MCU pin assigned. PG (pin 7) is open with an NC flag;
   option: wire it to a free STM32 input (PA10, PB2, PB5, PC14/15, PD5/6) on ESC_MCU (owner, other sheet).
7. **Inductor footprint**: TMPC1265HP land pattern (pads 3.1 x 5.0, 8.0 gap, 14.2 overall) fits inside the existing
   `L_Sunlord_MWSA1265S` pads (3.25 x 5.5, 8.0 gap, 14.5): footprint kept, placement and copper kept.
8. **Input HF cap**: TI uses 10 nF 0402/0603 at VIN-PGND; kept 100 nF/100 V 0603 (C524, in stock), placed right on
   the VIN finger 27 and PGND pin 26.
9. The U511 block is generated by `bl/aux.py` into **Brakes.kicad_sch** (not Power).

## 3. F1 / J510 path at 8 A
- **F1 (10 A, 58 V ATO)**: input at 8 A out is about 6.0 A at 36 V and 8.6 A at 25 V (with about 0.7 A of logic) ->
  86 % of rating at a low pack: OK for minutes, marginal for hours. 6 A continuous needs at most 6.6 A: fine.
  **F1 stays 10 A for the 6 A / 8 A short-term rating; change it to 15 A only if 8 A continuous is wanted** (Power
  sheet, not changed). Q1 SQJ459EP: about 1.7 W at 8.6 A (1.1 W at 7 A per the old note): give it copper.
- **+24V to J510**: In2 +24V plane, about 20 mm wide from the R538 / C531 vias down to J510 (1 oz): fine. **Weak
  spot**: J510 pin 1 is connected to that plane only by thermal spokes (0.4 mm, In2) and has no outer-layer +24V copper.
  Before fab: set J510 pad 1 (and pad 2, GND) to a solid zone connection or add an F/B +24V pour to J510 (planes.py /
  pours.py; owner, not done).
- J510 (Phoenix MKDS 1,5, 13.5 A IEC) itself is fine; LCSC stock is only 197.
